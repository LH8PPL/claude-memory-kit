- Self-review: author documents findings
- Independent pass: separate reviewer re-evaluates and categorizes findings
- Categories: B (Bugfix), I (Implementation), M (Missing)
- Notation: 3B+2I+3M means 3 bugs, 2 implementations, 3 missing items

**Why:** Ensures consistent review quality and explicit classification of change types across both passes

**How to apply:** Apply B/I/M notation when recording review findings from independent pass
