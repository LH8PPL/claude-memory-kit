Major decisions (feature posture, constraints, rationale) are recorded in ADR entries (e.g., ADR-0023) or decision entries (e.g., D-436, D-438). These form a durable record of what was decided and why.

**Why:** Future sessions need to know what was tried, what failed, and why the current state exists.

**How to apply:** When a decision is made (especially feature defaults or architectural pivots), record it in an ADR or D-NNN entry with evidence and rationale.
